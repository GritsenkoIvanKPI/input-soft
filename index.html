How it works

We are simplifying the process of getting started

Whichever solution you choose, you only need four steps to get started — and our team guides you through each of them during demo and implementation.

  1. Enter your parameters, rules and work shift limitations, then upload a flight schedule — or we will connect you to any available schedule platform.

  2. Get a ready-made flight plan, shift plan and roster for all your employees, or capture every service directly from the ramp. Change any parameter and calculate any number of what-if scenarios.

  3. Receive real-time flight schedule updates about any delays and share updated assignments and tasks with your employees in our app or via WhatsApp.

  4. Receive analytical reports with FTE, heads, paid and worked hours and more. Download them, send them automatically to colleagues or other software, or tailor them with our experts.
